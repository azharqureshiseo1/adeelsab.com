This policy explains how AdeelSab (<TodoMark label="Registered company name" />) handles information collected through **adeelsab.com**. It covers this website and the pre-launch waitlist only, not the marketplace itself, which will have its own policy.

## Information we collect

### When you join the waitlist

- Full name
- WhatsApp number
- City
- How you want to join (local seller, reseller, dropshipper or other)
- Optional: main product category, monthly order volume and any message you write
- Your language setting (English or Urdu) and the page you submitted the form from

### Automatically

- **A one-way hash of your IP address.** It is used to stop spam and repeated submissions. We never store your raw IP address.
- **Your language preference**, saved in your own browser (local storage) so the site remembers English or Urdu. It is not sent to us.
- **Analytics.** If enabled, Google Analytics 4 collects information about how the site is used, such as pages visited and device type, using cookies. We configure it to anonymise IP addresses.

## How we use it

- To contact you on WhatsApp about selling on AdeelSab, onboarding and launch
- To prepare your store if you become a seller
- To prevent spam and abuse of the waitlist form
- To understand which pages are useful and improve the site

We do not sell your information.

## Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers to run the website:

- **Supabase**, the database where waitlist submissions are stored
- **Hostinger**, for website hosting and email notifications
- **Google**, for analytics, if enabled

These providers may store data on servers outside Pakistan. We share information with authorities only when the law requires it.
