The **Seller Agreement** is the contract between AdeelSab and each seller. The full agreement is shared with you during onboarding. You'll have time to read it and ask questions before you sign.

This page summarises what the agreement covers, so you know what to expect. **The summary is not the agreement.** If anything here differs from the signed agreement, the signed agreement applies.

## What the agreement covers

### 1. Who can sell

Eligibility, identity verification (CNIC) and payout account requirements. The account must be in the seller's own name or the registered business's name.

### 2. Listing standards

Accurate titles, descriptions and photos. Prohibited items include counterfeit goods, illegal products and anything restricted by Pakistani law. The full list of prohibited and restricted items is in the agreement.

### 3. Pricing, commission and fees

Commission rates by category and every other fee, as published on the [pricing page](/pricing). How changes to fees are notified in advance.

### 4. Orders and dispatch

How orders are received, how quickly they must be packed and handed over, and what happens if an order has to be cancelled.

### 5. Delivery, COD and RTO

How delivery is arranged, who pays shipping, cash-on-delivery remittance, and RTO charges, as published on the [delivery page](/delivery).

### 6. Payouts and holds

The payout schedule, minimum payout, deductions, and when earnings can be held, as published on the [payouts page](/payouts).

### 7. Returns and disputes

Customer return rules, how disputes are decided, and the evidence sellers can provide. See [returns and disputes](/seller-hub/returns-and-disputes).

### 8. Founding Seller terms

For Founding Sellers: the reduced commission rate and period, free listing creation, and other program benefits. See the [Founding Seller Program](/founding-seller).

### 9. Reseller-Enabled Listings

How sellers opt in per listing, set margins, and switch reselling off. See [Reseller-Enabled Listings](/reseller-listings).

### 10. Data and confidentiality

How customer information is handled, and the rule that sellers must not use customer details outside the platform.

### 11. Ending the agreement

How either side can end the agreement, notice periods, and what happens to pending orders and payouts.

## Questions

Ask our seller team on WhatsApp during onboarding. We'll go through any clause with you in Urdu or English.
