Cash on delivery is how most Pakistani customers prefer to buy online. They pay only when the parcel is in their hands. The trade-off is **RTO (return to origin)**: some parcels are refused or undelivered and come back to you.

You can't bring RTO to zero. You can bring it down a lot, and every parcel you save protects your margin.

## What one refused parcel really costs

An RTO parcel costs more than the charge on our [delivery page](/delivery#rto):

- **The RTO charge** for the return trip
- **Your packaging**, which usually can't be reused
- **Lost selling time.** The stock was out of your shop for days while it could have sold to someone else.
- **Risk of damage** from travelling both ways

This is why a small improvement in your RTO rate often matters more than a small change in price.

## Why COD parcels get refused

1. **Impulse orders.** The customer ordered late at night and changed their mind by delivery day.
2. **Slow dispatch.** The longer a parcel takes, the more likely the customer has bought elsewhere or forgotten.
3. **Expectation mismatch.** Photos or descriptions made the product look different, and the customer refuses at the door.
4. **Wrong or incomplete address.** The rider can't find the house or reach the phone number.
5. **Unreachable customer.** Phone switched off, or nobody home after repeated attempts.
6. **Cash not ready.** The customer didn't have the amount when the rider arrived.
7. **Fake or prank orders.** Less common, but it happens.

## What you control

### Dispatch the same day

Speed is the single biggest lever. Orders that leave on the day they're placed reach customers while they still want the product. Keep stock accurate so you never need to delay or cancel.

### Make the listing match reality

Most doorstep refusals come from surprise. Use your own clear photos, show every side, list the material and measurements, and add a size chart. See [how to list products](/seller-hub/how-to-list-products) and [phone photography](/seller-hub/product-photography-with-a-phone).

### Price clearly

The price the customer saw should be the price they pay at the door. If you charge for delivery, make sure it's clear in the listing so the total isn't a surprise.

### Pack so it arrives perfect

A crushed box gives a hesitant customer a reason to refuse. Follow the [packaging guide](/seller-hub/packaging-guide).

### Watch for warning signs

Tell our seller team on WhatsApp if you see a pattern, such as repeated orders to the same address that are always refused. Don't contact customers outside the platform or share their details.

## Track your RTO rate

Your RTO rate is simple to calculate:

**RTO rate = parcels returned undelivered ÷ parcels dispatched × 100**

If you dispatched 200 parcels this month and 24 came back, your RTO rate is 24 ÷ 200 × 100 = **12%**.

Track it every month, and break it down:

- **By product.** A high RTO on one product usually points to a listing problem.
- **By city.** Some areas take longer to deliver to, and slower delivery means more refusals.
- **By price.** Higher-priced COD orders are refused more often, so be extra careful with their listings and packing.

## A 30-day plan

1. **Week 1.** Start a simple log of every RTO: product, city, price and reason if known.
2. **Week 2.** Fix the three listings with the most RTOs: new photos, clearer descriptions, size charts.
3. **Week 3.** Make same-day dispatch your rule. Pack orders before you close the shop.
4. **Week 4.** Compare your RTO rate with last month, and repeat with the next worst products.
