AdeelSab is a Pakistani marketplace made up entirely of independent sellers. We don't buy or hold stock. You keep your products in your shop or warehouse, and when a customer orders, we pick up the parcel, deliver it, collect the cash and pay you.

This guide walks you through the whole journey, from joining the waitlist to shipping your first order.

## Who can sell

AdeelSab is opening in stages. Right now we are onboarding **local sellers**: people who already hold stock and can pack orders.

- Shop owners in markets and bazaars
- Wholesalers and distributors
- Manufacturers and local brands
- Home-based businesses with consistent stock

If you don't hold stock yourself, the reseller program is for you. It opens after launch, once the catalogue has enough products. You can [join the reseller waitlist](/sell/resellers) today.

## What to prepare

You don't need a website, an app or a photo studio. Keep these ready before your onboarding call:

1. **Your CNIC.** Clear photos of the front and back.
2. **A payout account in your name.** A bank account (IBAN), JazzCash or Easypaisa.
3. **Product photos.** A phone camera is enough. See our guide to [product photography with a phone](/seller-hub/product-photography-with-a-phone).
4. **A price list.** Your selling price for each item, and how many you have in stock.

An NTN is not required to join the waitlist. Read [NTN and tax basics](/seller-hub/ntn-and-tax-basics) to understand when it helps.

## From waitlist to first order

### 1. Join the waitlist

Fill in the [waitlist form](/founding-seller#waitlist). It takes under a minute. Sellers who join before launch are considered for the [Founding Seller Program](/founding-seller).

### 2. Onboarding call

Our seller team contacts you on WhatsApp. We explain how orders, delivery, commission and payouts work, in Urdu or English, and answer your questions. Nothing is signed on this call.

### 3. Verify and sign

We verify your CNIC and payout account. You read and sign the seller agreement. Take your time with it, and ask us about anything that isn't clear.

### 4. Listings go live

Send your photos and prices on WhatsApp. For Founding Sellers, our team writes the titles and descriptions and sets up each listing. You check the prices and stock before anything goes live.

### 5. Your first order

When an order comes in, you are notified. Pack it carefully (see the [packaging guide](/seller-hub/packaging-guide)) and hand it over when the rider or courier arrives. Delivery and cash collection are handled by us, and you are paid on the published payout schedule.

## Tips for your first month

- **Start with your best sellers.** List the products that already sell well in your shop, with accurate stock.
- **Dispatch quickly.** Parcels that leave on the day of the order are refused less often.
- **Be exact in descriptions.** Wrong sizes and misleading photos are the main causes of returns.
- **Watch your returns.** Our guide on [reducing RTO](/seller-hub/reducing-rto-in-cod) explains what drives refused parcels and how to cut them.
