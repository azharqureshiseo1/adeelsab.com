When people hear that AdeelSab will support local sellers, resellers and dropshippers, the first question is usually: *why not open all three at once?*

The answer comes from one decision we made early. **AdeelSab doesn't buy or hold stock.** Every product on the marketplace will come from an independent seller.

## No stock means no shortcut

Many marketplaces launch by buying inventory themselves, so the shelves look full on day one. We chose not to. Holding stock would put us in competition with the sellers we want to serve, and it would tie up money in warehouses instead of in delivery, support and payouts.

The trade-off is that the catalogue can only grow as fast as sellers join.

## What that means for resellers

A reseller earns by selling products that other sellers have listed. They share a product with their customers, place the order, and earn the margin the seller has published.

That only works if there is something worth sharing. A reseller program with a thin catalogue wastes the reseller's time. They'd find a few products, struggle to match their customers' needs, and leave.

So we're being honest about sequencing:

1. **Local sellers first.** Shops, wholesalers and brands with real stock. They build the catalogue.
2. **Resellers next.** Once the catalogue is deep enough to be worth a reseller's effort, the program opens. The [waitlist](/sell/resellers) is open now.
3. **Dropshippers in Phase 2.** Store integrations and stock sync come after the marketplace is running. See [what's coming](/sell/dropshippers).

## Why this is better for local sellers too

Sellers who join first don't just get [Founding Seller benefits](/founding-seller). When reselling opens, their products are already listed. They can switch on [Reseller-Enabled Listings](/reseller-listings) and reach customers through resellers across the country, on margins they set themselves.

## What we won't do

We won't tell resellers that products are available before they are, and we won't publish numbers we don't have. When each program opens, we'll say so plainly and message everyone on the waitlist.

If you run a shop, a wholesale business or a local brand, [this is the moment to join](/founding-seller).
